# About Me

I am an **Assistant Professor (Profesor Ayudante)** in the Department of Radiology and Rehabilitation at the Faculty of Medicine, **Universidad Complutense de Madrid (UCM)**. Currently, I am completing my Ph.D. in Medico-Surgical Research under the supervision of Dr. Diego García Pinto, focusing on the development and simulation of **X-ray Phase-Contrast Imaging (XPCI)** systems using diffraction gratings.

## Research Interests
* **Advanced Medical Imaging:** X-ray Phase-Contrast, Dark-Field Imaging, and Talbot-Lau Interferometry.
* **Monte Carlo Simulations:** Architecture optimizations for radiological setups utilizing the **GAMOS** (Geant4-based) framework.
* **Computational Radiology:** Application of deep learning frameworks (e.g., Generative Adversarial Networks) to remove artifacts and improve image reconstruction.

## Funding & Projects
I actively participate as a research team member in the **PREDICO Project** (*Experimentos preclínicos para el desarrollo y optimización de nuevas modalidades de imagen para cáncer de mama*, Ref: PID2021-123390OB-C22), funded by the Spanish Ministry of Science, Innovation, and Universities.
